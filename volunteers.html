Potential Partners

We welcome partnerships with organizations dedicated to empowering women both in Kenya and internationally

Government Agencies

Ministry of Gender, County Governments, National Gender and Equality Commission - partnering for policy implementation and community programs

Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs)

Local and international NGOs focused on women's rights, education, health, and economic empowerment

Private Sector Partners

Corporations and businesses interested in CSR initiatives, women entrepreneurship support, and skill development programs

International Development Agencies

UN Women, USAID, EU development programs, and other international organizations supporting women's empowerment in Africa

Educational Institutions

Universities, vocational training centers, and educational organizations for skills training and capacity building partnerships

Community Based Organizations

Women's groups, self-help groups, and community organizations working at grassroots level across Kenya
