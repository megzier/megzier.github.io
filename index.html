About us

Empowering Women, Transforming Lives

Rachum International Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to women empowerment in Kenya. We provide entrepreneurship training, financial literacy education, and leadership development to help women achieve social and economic independence.

Why Choose Us

Committed to Women Empowerment

To empower communities by mobilizing resources, advancing education, health, and environmental sustainability, and building partnerships that drive lasting social and economic impact.
A future where women and communities thrive with dignity, resilience, and opportunity—where sustainable development transforms lives across Kenya and beyond.
