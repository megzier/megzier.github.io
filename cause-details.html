Our Programs & Impact

Comprehensive initiatives transforming lives across Kenya

Entrepreneurship Training

Empowering women with essential business skills and resources to start and grow sustainable enterprises. Our comprehensive training covers all aspects of running a successful business in Kenya's dynamic market.

Program Objectives:

  • Business planning and strategy development tailored to local markets
  • Marketing and customer acquisition techniques
  • Financial management, record keeping, and tax compliance
  • Access to startup capital through microfinance partnerships
  • Ongoing mentorship and business advisory support
Impact & Results

Over 150 women have started their own businesses through this program, creating more than 400 jobs in local communities. Success rate of 78% after 2 years in business.

Financial Literacy

Essential financial knowledge enabling women to manage resources effectively, build savings, and achieve economic independence through proven money management strategies.

Program Objectives:

  • Personal budgeting and household financial planning
  • Savings strategies and investment opportunities
  • Understanding mobile banking, M-Pesa, and digital finance
  • Group savings schemes and Table Banking initiatives
  • Credit management and debt reduction strategies
Impact & Results

Participants report a 60% average increase in savings within 6 months. Improved household financial stability reported by 85% of program graduates.

Leadership Development

Building confident women leaders who can drive change in their communities, advocate for women's rights, and inspire the next generation of female leaders across Kenya.

Program Objectives:

  • Leadership skills development and public speaking training
  • Community organizing and grassroots mobilization
  • Advocacy training and women's rights awareness
  • Civic engagement and political participation support
  • Mentorship networks and peer support groups
Impact & Results

45 women have taken up leadership roles in their communities, with 12 successfully elected to local government positions. 200+ women actively engaged in civic activities.

Health & Wellness

Promoting women's health through education, access to quality healthcare services, and comprehensive wellness programs addressing physical and mental health needs.

Program Objectives:

  • Reproductive health education and family planning services
  • Maternal and child health support programs
  • Mental health counseling and psychosocial support
  • Nutrition education and wellness workshops
  • Health insurance access and medical referrals
Impact & Results

Over 300 women reached with health services, 40% improvement in maternal health outcomes. Mental health support provided to 120+ women with 90% reporting improved wellbeing.

Environmental Sustainability

Engaging women in sustainable environmental practices that protect our planet while creating economic opportunities through climate-smart agriculture and conservation.

Program Objectives:

  • Climate-smart agriculture and organic farming training
  • Tree planting campaigns and forest conservation
  • Sustainable energy solutions (solar panels, biogas)
  • Water conservation and rainwater harvesting techniques
  • Waste management and recycling initiatives
Impact & Results

5,000+ trees planted across 3 counties, 80 women trained in sustainable farming practices. Carbon footprint reduced by estimated 200 tons annually through our initiatives.

Vocational Training

Equipping women with practical vocational skills for sustainable income generation and economic empowerment through hands-on training and certification programs.

Program Objectives:

  • Tailoring, fashion design, and garment production
  • Food processing, catering, and hospitality services
  • Hairdressing, beauty therapy, and salon management
  • Handicrafts, beadwork, and traditional arts
  • Digital skills, computer literacy, and online marketing
Impact & Results

200+ women certified in various vocational skills with 70% reporting increased income within 6 months of completion. Average income increase of 150%.
