Women Empowerment Events

Past events and upcoming activities focused on empowering women across Kenya

Women's Health & Empowerment March

Date: March 2024 Nairobi

Community march advocating for women's health services and gender equality across Nairobi communities.

Beadwork Skills Training Workshop

Date: January 2024 Samburu

Traditional beadwork training for Maasai and Samburu women to enhance income generation opportunities.

Pottery & Entrepreneurship Training

Date: December 2023 Kisumu

Traditional pottery making combined with business skills development for sustainable livelihoods.

Women's Economic Empowerment Forum

Upcoming June 2025 - Nairobi

Regional forum bringing together women entrepreneurs from Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and Rwanda to share best practices.

EAC Women Leadership Summit

Upcoming August 2025 - Kampala

East African Community summit on women in leadership, policy-making, and community development across the region.

Rural Women Skills Training - Kisumu

Upcoming Quarterly 2025

Ongoing quarterly training program in Kisumu County providing vocational skills and business development support to rural women.
