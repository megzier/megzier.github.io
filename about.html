About us

Empowering Women, Transforming Communities

Rachum International Foundation is a non-profit company limited by guarantee, established to support charitable, educational, and community development initiatives. We focus on promoting economic empowerment, advancing health and education, and championing environmental sustainability for women in Kenya.
